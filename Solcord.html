Up and running
in a few steps.

The installer adds Solcord to your existing Discord desktop app.

Get the installer
  1. Download and extractDownload the installer package and extract it to a folder.
  2. Close Discord and installQuit Discord from the system tray, open SolcordInstaller.exe, and select your Discord installation.
  3. Open DiscordFind Solcord in settings to choose your theme and plugins.

Good to know.

Is this a separate Discord app?

Solcord is a mod for the existing Discord desktop app. It adds its own settings, plugins, and visual changes.

Can I turn the theme or startup screen off?

Yes. Solcord settings include a chat color switch and separate controls for the startup animation, music, and plugins.

What does the online counter measure?

Once connected, it will show participating Solcord installations that sent a heartbeat in the last three minutes. It measures open installations, including idle ones, rather than unique people. Visiting this website does not add you to the count.

The planned client setting is optional and sends a random installation ID. It does not need your Discord account, messages, or token.

Where can I see the code?

The project is on GitHub. Release packages include source and license notices.
